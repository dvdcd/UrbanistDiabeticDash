Diabetic Dash

Real-time CGM display for the Adafruit Matrix Portal S3

After installing

  1. Connect your device to power via USB-C.
  2. On your phone or computer, join the WiFi network DiabeticDash-Setup.
  3. Open 192.168.4.1 in your browser and enter your WiFi credentials and Dexcom login.
  4. Click Save & Restart — the dashboard will appear on the display within a minute.

Requires Chrome or Edge with a USB connection to the device. Source on GitHub
